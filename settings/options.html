Privacy & data

BeforeUClick separates ordinary app settings from optional information about the emails you check.

What this setting means: when it is off, BeforeUClick can still check the email you are viewing, but it does not retain known-sender addresses or a history of which emails were checked. When it is on, addresses you deliberately add to Known senders are kept locally, and the daily counter stores only an opaque message fingerprint plus the latest result so the same email is not counted twice.

  • App preferences: text size, contrast, panel size/position and similar settings are saved locally so the app remembers how you configured it. They are not email history.
  • Email content: bodies, subjects, recipients, analysed URLs, raw headers, extracted metadata and full reports are not kept as history by this option.
  • Network use: the current MVP analyses locally and has no BeforeUClick telemetry, advertising-data upload, or data-selling backend.
  • Reference data: the rules and reference lists are bundled read-only with the extension and do not require remembered email information.
